Gull Skerry · before dawn

Lighthouse Archipelago

You wake on wet sand with salt in your mouth and no memory of the night before. Your boat lies broken on the rocks behind you. All you have is a name, Mara, and a red light flashing far to the north.

Somewhere under that light is home. Every lighthouse between here and there has gone dark. Relight them to raise the old causeways, cross the islands, and find out how you ended up here.

Wrenhaven quay · morning

Home by the green light

Finn talks the whole way across. Ezra Corran never reached the harbor to confess. The sloop's men caught him rowing north, and Finn found him on the shingle at Puffin Reach with just enough breath left to tell the last secret.

The red lantern. For two winters the wreckers hung it at their masthead in the fog, right where a homeward sailor expects to see Wrenhaven. Every ship they took had been steering for home. So had you.

Mara is on the quay when the skiff comes in under her green light. She doesn't say "I told you so." She takes your sleeve the way she did the morning you left, and this time she doesn't let go.
